# Product principles

**BookMyProtest informs, connects and assists. It does not authorize, certify, represent, endorse or guarantee.**

BookMyProtest provides information, workflow tools and public listing features.
Authorities make authority decisions.
Organizers remain responsible for their events and the information they publish.

## The model

CREATE → PREPARE → PUBLISH → DISCOVER → SUPPORT / SHARE

- **Create / prepare** (private): the planning flow, the sourced checklist, documents, and the organizer's own permission tracker.
- **Publish** (a choice): a separate public page, created only by an explicit organizer action. It never depends on BookMyProtest saying an event is legally approved.
- **Discover / support / share** (public): anyone can browse; stateful actions need an account; organizers and the public see totals only.

## Rules the product follows

1. **Provenance on every public fact.** Organizer-provided, BookMyProtest-source-checked, official-source link, system-derived, or unknown. An organizer's claim is never styled or worded as verified by BookMyProtest. A calculated date says what it was calculated from.
2. **Status words are reported words.** Public permission status always reads "… — reported by organizer" (or "Status not provided"). No green, no badge, no "approved" in BookMyProtest's own voice.
3. **Preparation is not approval.** Internal readiness states are named *Preparation complete*, *Preparation complete with warnings*, *Action required*, *Status unknown*. A complete checklist never means "legally approved".
4. **"No conflict found" is not "available".** A listing is not an approval, and other listings say nothing about official capacity.
5. **Minimum data.** Supporters give an account and a choice; nobody (organizer, public, admin screens) can list who. No identity documents, no political, religious or caste fields, no supporter export, no attendee contact export.
6. **Neutral moderation.** Reasons are conduct and content only (violence or threat, harassment, doxxing, impersonation, fraud, illegal content, spam, other). There is no viewpoint category. Every action is logged and reversible.
7. **No money.** No payments, donations, fundraising or vendor checkout.
8. **Legal text is a draft until a lawyer reviews it.** `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/platform-guidelines` are marked *LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH*.

## The test for any new feature

Does it improve organizer preparation, public discovery, supporter information, status transparency, sharing, or reduce external effort? If not, it does not ship.
